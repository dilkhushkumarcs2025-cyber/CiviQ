How CiviQ Resolves Civic Problems

A rigorous 7-stage architecture ensuring absolute accountability and transparency[cite: 3].

01

Citizen Report

Citizens capture photos, exact GPS location, and descriptions of potholes, garbage, or broken streetlights[cite: 2, 3].

02

AI Intelligence

Computer Vision and NLP analyze images and text to categorize issues and detect duplicates instantly[cite: 3, 5].

03

Unifying Cases

Multiple reports of the same physical problem get clustered into a single master Public Issue[cite: 3].

04

Live Tracking

Citizens monitor real-time progress timelines, assigned departments, and status updates openly[cite: 3, 6].

06
